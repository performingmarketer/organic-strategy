# Example: Kerala digital marketing training institute (hypothetical plan)

**Not a real site audit.** This example uses fictional/provisional business details as a workflow demonstration, not confirmed user claims or actual search volume/traffic.

- Business: practical digital marketing training institute, Thrissur District, Kerala
- Audience: recent course graduates seeking hands-on confidence; business owners seeking usable marketing skills
- Goal: qualified course enquiries and informed counselling, no placement guarantees
- Language: Malayalam with English marketing terms
- Assets: sample teaching clips, approved tutor notes, genuine course syllabus (request real evidence)
- Website: **not provided — no technical audit or ranking claims**

## Customer-driven SEO hypotheses

| Topic/query hypothesis | Intent | Proposed page type | Original proof needed | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| digital marketing practical training Thrissur | local/service | course landing page | curriculum, schedule, location | syllabus enquiry |
| what practical assignments are in a digital marketing course | informational | FAQ/tutorial | actual exercises | ask about training |
| digital marketing course vs internship | comparison | decision guide | objective definitions, examples | explore options |
| Meta Ads training: what to practise first | informational | tutorial | sample account screenshots with sensitive info removed | related course info |

**No keyword volumes or rankings are asserted.** Prioritize high-intent landing content before producing dozens of general blogs; check existing pages and GSC first.

## Social content pillars

1. **Skill demonstration:** instructor builds a campaign briefing from a mock client.
2. **Student decision support:** questions to ask before joining any course.
3. **Practical mistakes:** targeting and measurement errors new marketers commonly make, with examples.
4. **Mentor explanation:** responsible expectations for internships, skills and portfolios.
5. **Customer/business owner angle:** how to evaluate a marketing campaign report (without claiming easy sales).
