# Competitive benchmark — ethical differentiation, not an originality claim

This is an original decision framework for Organic Strategy. Reference third-party projects only to compare functionality and identify useful design patterns. Do not copy their prompt text, branding, claims or unpublished work. Check exact licensing before reusing source code.

## Why this module exists

Several public AI skills already offer SEO planning, local SEO, social calendars and repurposing. “We combine SEO and social” by itself is **not** a defensible unique claim. Prefer a narrower useful positioning: **local customer intent + Malayalam-native content + one evidence-backed Search-to-Social-to-Enquiry journey.** This is a project *positioning choice*, not proof no one else offers similar features.

## Business-level competitor review workflow

1. Identify legitimate competitors or comparable organizations **only from supplied names/URLs or real research**. No invented business listings.
2. Scope channel: website search page vs Maps/GBP vs Instagram/Facebook, separating surfaces and local context.
3. Capture URL, observation date, actual visible offering, customer question addressed, evidence format, CTA and the audience segment. Account-private data requires user-supplied permission/access.
4. Build a content-gap matrix: **what customer question is still unanswered**, what firsthand proof user can produce, how to improve audience usefulness, where to publish, source certainty and effort.
5. Ignore competitor content volume as an automatic target; do not plagiarize or claim ranking causal insights from a screenshot.
6. Recommend up to three differentiation experiments that a small team can realistically execute.

## Originality and QA checks

- At least one example, demonstrable skill or real local fact per priority topic, or a flagged evidence gap.
- No near-duplicate location pages or mass AI rewrites.
- One original source card to multiple purpose-different formats, preserving verified claims.
- Business outcome focus: qualified actions, not fabricated vanity metrics.
