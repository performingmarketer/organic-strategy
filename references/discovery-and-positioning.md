# Discovery, customer psychology and positioning

## Ask only what is necessary

Inputs: company, business model, exact offer, audience, customer location/language, primary goal, buyer journey, differentiators and demonstrable proof, current website/pages, social channels, preferred format, team bandwidth and available analytics. Reuse conversation history. Avoid a long interrogation; collect missing details in a short follow-up and proceed when possible.

## Multi-stage buying journey

| Stage | Customer question | Search opportunity | Social opportunity | Business objective |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Why does this problem happen? | explanatory guide | short pain-point diagnosis | qualified awareness |
| Problem-aware | What are my options? | how-to / options page | comparison carousel / demo | informed demand |
| Solution-aware | Which provider/product fits? | category/service page | behind the scenes, proof, Q&A | trust and consideration |
| Ready to act | What are the details and next steps? | service, pricing and location pages | transparent process, FAQ and CTA | qualified enquiry / booking |
| Existing customer | How do I use or improve it? | support/FAQ/helpful guide | community, tips, aftercare | repeat value / referral |

## Message and content matrix

Record segment, real pain point/question, desired change, obstacles, unique real proof, suggested helpful page, social hook, action and metric. Don't make up testimonials or assume demographics. Distinguish actual consumer voice (quoted with permission) from plausible hypotheses.

## Positioning exercise

Define one-sentence service promise, top 3 credible differentiators, proof gaps, local relevance, terms customers actually use, conversion friction, and commonly asked questions. Identify topics where the business can provide unique demonstrations, first-hand expertise, processes, data, or examples.

## Capacity tiers (illustrative, not prescriptions)

- Solo operator / very constrained: improve one highest-intent page, publish 1–2 thoughtful social posts per week, update one FAQ/source-of-truth.
- Small team: review 2 priority pages monthly, create 2–4 substantive web assets/refreshes, publish 2–4 posts weekly, monitor questions and replies.
- Larger team: scale only where production maintains quality; assign writers/reviewers/designers and track throughput. No minimum quantity needed to rank or grow.

## Evidence labels

**Checked** = directly inspected with named URL/source/date. **User-provided** = supplied by the user, not independently validated. **Hypothesis** = probable but unverified. **To verify** = check with user/tools. Never present an inference as an audit finding.
