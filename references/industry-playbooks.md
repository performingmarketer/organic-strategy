# Industry starter playbooks — hypotheses, not observed market data

Adapt to any business beyond this library. These 10 sectors are starting points inspired by the Ad Strategy category range; never imply an industry list controls platform targeting or Google rankings.

| Sector | Examples | Search-intent opportunity | Social pillar / unique evidence | Conversion action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Education & training | digital marketing, vocational, preschools, coaching, universities | course-specific landing, curriculum, comparisons, location | class demonstrations, actual assignments, candid mentor FAQ | admission counselling |
| Healthcare & fitness | clinics, dentists, labs, yoga, gyms | care/service facts, FAQ, booking/location | accurate general education with qualified review | booked appointment |
| Retail & e-commerce | fashion, jewellery, furniture, electronics, online stores | category/product descriptions, fit/returns | real product demos, care instructions | purchase |
| Food & hospitality | restaurants, bakeries, hotels, homestays, tourism | menu/property/service/location | actual atmosphere, preparation, guest details with permission | booking/visit/order |
| Property & home | real estate, interiors, construction, plumbing, repair | service/location pages, process comparisons | real project walkthrough, scope and materials | qualified site visit |
| Automotive | dealers, garages, car detailing, accessories | model/service pages, maintenance questions | real vehicle care demonstration | test drive / appointment |
| Professional & B2B | agencies, accounting, HR, consultants, law | service/how-it-works and decision guides | expert explainers, process, case evidence | consultation |
| Events & beauty | wedding planners, photography, salons, makeup | style/service/package pages | real portfolio with consent, process BTS | booking |
| Technology, industrial & logistics | software, IT, manufacturing, supply chain | solution pages, integration guides, buyer comparisons | verified process demos and technical explainers | qualified demo |
| Agriculture & community | farm inputs, nurseries, NGOs, regional events | how-to/local availability/service | field demos, transparent outcomes, community Q&A | enquiry / registration |

## Segment notes

- For YMYL (health, finance, legal), require qualified expert review and source-backed claims; never offer individualized diagnoses or guaranteed outcomes from marketing content.
- For minors/schools, protect identities and get permission before using images or testimonials.
- For Kerala businesses, Malayalam + natural English terminology may fit, but audience/region language preference matters more than assumptions.
- For local businesses, distinguish actual serving area from location-keyword doorway pages.
- If a business has only social and no website, produce social-first output and SEO setup options rather than pretending an audit occurred.
