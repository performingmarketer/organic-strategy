# Kerala Local Organic Growth — business-model-aware SEO playbook (v1.1)

**Goal:** help genuine nearby customers discover an eligible, relevant business, understand services, and choose an accurate next step. This is not a promise of rankings, proximity or local-pack inclusion.

## Gate 0 — establish the business reality

Record with evidence status: business model (customer-facing storefront / service-area business visiting customers / hybrid / online-only); actual staffed locations; whether customers can visit; verified GBP profile URL/status; eligible business name/category/service; correct public phone/site; actual service reach; public opening hours; customer language and target regions. A service area is NOT an additional storefront. An online-only business should not be assumed eligible for GBP.

**Privacy:** do not expose a private home address, or advise using virtual offices/co-working spaces as fake staffed business locations. If eligibility is unclear, check Google's current guidelines before suggesting edits.

## Gate 1 — three different surfaces; don't conflate them

| Surface | Evidence or checks | Helpful response |
|---|---|---|
| Google Maps / Local Pack | profile eligibility, precise query and searched place, relevance/distance/prominence context, correct category, contact details, reviews | Fix factual info and user decision path; distance is not controllable via keyword stuffing |
| Organic website results | GSC query/page data, real website/service pages, indexing/canonical/internal links if inspected | Match intent with a verified page, unique evidence, local context and a clear service CTA |
| Organic social discovery | actual Instagram/Facebook/YouTube posts and insights, frequently asked customer questions, meaningful DMs | Helpful location-relevant media, natural language, community interactions and routed enquiries |

Never treat a single search screenshot as a verified district-wide ranking, or infer geographic demand/search volume from a keyword list. Google's local ranking documentation highlights relevance, distance and prominence (often described as popularity); do not claim a specific weight.

## Gate 2 — local intent map (use Malayalam + English naturally)

Produce rows for *specific buyer questions*, not cloned city keyword pages. Each row includes:
- Language of **hypothesized search wording**: Malayalam script, natural English, Manglish transliteration where audience evidence justifies it. Do not pretend the hypotheses are measured keywords.
- Intent (discover, compare, book/visit, support), customer type, location truth, existing/proposed target URL, original local proof required, primary CTA, and verification needed.
- Query group **must fit an actual business**. Example hypotheses for an Irinjalakuda training provider: `digital marketing course Irinjalakuda`, `ഡിജിറ്റൽ മാർക്കറ്റിംഗ് കോഴ്സ് തൃശൂർ`, `practical digital marketing training Thrissur`. They are brainstorming inputs, NOT ranked/volume-confirmed terms.

## Gate 3 — accurate location and service information

- **Business Profile field review:** existing value, proposed change, evidence source, user impact, implementation permission. Review legitimate category, services, description, phone, website, hours, appointment pathway, accurate images and verifiable review process. Do not invent a GBP status or feature availability.
- **Local landing page brief:** actual services, service eligibility, location, address/contact as publicly appropriate, who serves the area, original projects/instructor evidence, frequently asked decision questions, travel/visit details if applicable, page title/H1 and helpful internal links. City/area copy must contain something **substantially unique**; do not programmatically clone 40 city pages or fabricate offices.
- **Reviews:** request unbiased feedback from genuine customers without incentives, gating or fake accounts; respond politely without exposing their private details. Do not assert that more GBP posts directly cause a specific rank improvement.
- **Citations and relationships:** prioritize factual contact consistency and legitimate local organizations, events, partnerships or earned coverage. Avoid purchased spam listings and link schemes.

## Gate 4 — produce a 4-week Local Growth Sprint

Choose at most 3 meaningful fixes and 1–2 content experiments before scaling, adapted to bandwidth:
1. **Correctness** — verified profile, business info and enquiry channel.
2. **High-intent page** — improve the real course/service page (not a generic blog substitute).
3. **Local proof** — capture a real class/demo/project/facility/service experience (consent needed).
4. **One customer question** — publish a useful FAQ/article with one contextual Reel/Carousel and an accurate CTA.
5. **Track** — baseline GSC/GBP/GA4/social metrics where accessible and business enquiries in CRM; distinguish direct vs self-reported/assisted.
