# Malayalam-first organic content and reel editorial QA

Malayalam market focus is a **content-design capability**, not a claim that one language automatically ranks better. Ask whether the audience speaks Malayalam, English or a mix; choose audience-first wording and regional details only when supported.

## Natural-language workflow

1. Record primary audience, literacy/comfort with Malayalam script, location and professional vocabulary. Default to Malayalam prose with familiar English terms (`SEO`, `Google Business Profile`, `Meta Ads`, `Reel`, `WhatsApp`) where they sound natural.
2. Distinguish **content language** from **candidate search-language forms**. Generate Malayalam-script queries and transliterated/English possibilities only as **unverified hypotheses** until GSC/keyword evidence exists.
3. Use a credible one-question hook anchored to the audience's actual problem. Do not start every piece with identical “നിങ്ങൾക്കറിയാമോ?”, generic guarantees or fabricated statistics.
4. Explain **what happens → why → one actionable demonstration → honest next step**. Prefer complete natural spoken sentences; do not pepper every sentence with marketing jargon.
5. Confirm every Malayalam spelling and punctuation decision; keep brand names untouched. Avoid awkward direct translations or invented local colloquialisms.
6. No fabricated student experiences, customer messages, screenshots, job-placement, rankings, revenue or testimonials; ask for a real demonstration if credibility is essential.
7. Mention location accurately, e.g., Irinjalakuda is within Thrissur district; do **not** imply verified office or service presence in every town merely because content targets a town.

## Reel deliverable (30–60 seconds or user duration)

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Audience + one problem | Single clear person/need, stage, local relevance only if real |
| Hook choices | 3 **meaningfully different** openings: problem, objection, practical demonstration |
| Spoken script | Natural Malayalam, precise English marketing words; estimate timing by **reading aloud** and user delivery pace |
| Shots / evidence | Real footage or clearly labeled illustrative mockup; 1 short on-screen point per scene |
| Caption | Useful, accurate context; no dependence on misleading hashtags |
| CTA | Save/use a real checklist, visit an actual link, ask a relevant question, contact business if available |
| QA | Spelling, user facts, consent, format length, subtitles, claims, placement-no-guarantees |

Do not promise an exact 30s run time solely from a rough word count. Provide a shorter alternate if speech is dense. For automated video, preserve any supplied logos exactly as provided; never redraw/recolor without permission.

## Hook bank — original examples, adapt rather than copy blindly

- Problem diagnosis: “Google-ൽ നിങ്ങളുടെ Service അന്വേഷിക്കുന്നവർക്കു website കണ്ടെത്താൻ എളുപ്പമാണോ?”
- Decision aid: “Digital Marketing Institute തിരഞ്ഞെടുക്കുന്നതിന് മുമ്പ് ഈ മൂന്ന് ചോദ്യങ്ങൾ ചോദിക്കൂ.”
- Demonstration: “ഒരു Customer ചോദിക്കുന്ന ചോദ്യം എങ്ങനെ Blog-ഉം Reel-ഉം ആക്കാം?”
- Common misconception: “Instagram-ൽ Reel Post ചെയ്താൽ മാത്രം Google Ranking കൂടുമോ? അത്ര ലളിതമല്ല.”
- Business owner: “Views കിട്ടുന്നു; പക്ഷേ ശരിയായ Enquiries വരാൻ എന്ത് മാറ്റണം?”

## One source, three distinct angles (illustration)

**Verified original source when available:** a business's own practical-training lesson outline; until supplied, use this only as a hypothetical example.

- Website FAQ: learning outcomes and what hands-on activities actually include — search/decision angle.
- Reel: filmed exercise and one visible learning step — demonstration angle.
- Carousel: a checklist to evaluate institutes — comparison/decision angle.

Do not create testimonial quotes or transform an example into a claimed result. Don't repeat the same headline three times with superficial format changes.
