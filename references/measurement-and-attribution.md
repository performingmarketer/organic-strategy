# Organic growth measurement and learning loop

## SEO metrics

- **GSC impressions:** appearances reported by Search Console, not people who viewed page.
- **GSC clicks:** clicks from search result surfaces counted under its definitions.
- **Organic CTR:** GSC clicks / GSC impressions × 100. Report query/page/device/country filters and date range where relevant.
- **Average position:** an aggregate estimate, not a real-time guaranteed rank for everyone.
- **Organic engaged session / conversion:** defined via GA4/analytics if available and configured; distinguish analytics attribution from GSC clicks.
- **Qualified organic lead rate:** verified qualified organic enquiries / all tracked organic enquiries × 100; CRM qualification definition required.
- **Local:** Google Business Profile interactions available in its Performance reporting; do not invent Maps ranking data.

## Organic social metrics

- **Reach** and **views/impressions** are platform-defined and not interchangeable.
- **Engagement rate:** explicitly state denominator (interactions / reach ×100 or interactions / followers ×100 as chosen); never compare different formulas silently.
- **Saves/shares/comments** when surfaced; avoid counting bot/spam activity as real demand.
- **Retention/average watch time:** only when analytics provides it, and define what the metric means on the platform.
- **Profile actions, qualified DMs, calls, website visits, meetings, conversions:** verify source as far as tools and privacy permit.

## Business scorecard

| Goal | Leading indicator | Business outcome | Source | Review cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Local service bookings | calls / FAQ page traffic | attended qualified appointments | GBP + analytics + CRM | monthly |
| Training admissions | course page clicks + informed messages | admissions | GSC + GA4 + CRM | monthly |
| Retail sales | product page engagement, social enquiries | verified purchases & margin | analytics + commerce/CRM | monthly |
| Expert authority | helpful content usage, qualified inquiries | meaningful projects | analytics + CRM | monthly/quarterly |

## Sample learning loop

1. Record baseline dates, existing pages, posts, tracked actions, costs/time. If unavailable, write **No baseline available**.
2. Publish high-quality, distinctive content with clear hypothesis and owner.
3. Distribute in relevant owned communities and channels; do not spam.
4. Review real platform analytics and business outcomes; for small samples avoid confident causal claims.
5. Decide: keep, enrich, improve a page, change a hook/format, consolidate duplicate topics, or stop producing low-value content.
6. Document hypothesis and evidence; avoid claiming guaranteed time-to-rank. SEO time horizons vary substantially; social signals can be faster but are volatile.

## Qualified enquiry and assisted-channel tracking

Use `qualified-enquiry-analytics.md` for a non-identifying lead ledger, source confidence, deduplication and qualified lead definition. Qualified enquiries are observed business outcomes, never inferred from likes, GSC clicks or DMs without confirmed criteria. Track direct (where instrumented), self-reported, assisted and unknown carefully.

## Attribution caveats

Direct messaging, untagged links, organic platform dark traffic, zero-click search and multi-touch journeys obscure attribution. Use UTM-tagged URLs when supported, simple “How did you find us?” questions, and CRM lead-source notes (with consent). Do not claim organic social posting automatically increases Google ranking.
