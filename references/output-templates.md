# Output templates and delivery rules

## Full integrated strategy

**v1.1 required additions:** clearly separate Maps/GBP, organic website and organic social when local; provide a Source of Truth Card (with proof/permission), at least one adapted Malayalam Reel and Carousel when Malayalam requested, and a qualified enquiry definition with attribution confidence. Work to capacity, not daily-volume requirements.

1. Business snapshot + what has been verified vs user-supplied vs assumptions
2. Search/customer question matrix by segment, stage, intent, conversion value
3. Keyword and page map (existing/proposed URLs explicitly labeled)
4. SEO action priorities: technical verification, existing pages, topic clusters, internal links, local/GBP where applicable
5. Organic social pillars & proof inventory with truly differentiated angles
6. 30-day integrated calendar (Week/Day; SEO task; social asset; CTA; owner; KPI)
7. 2 SEO briefs, 2 polished Reel scripts and 1 carousel concept (unless user requests only strategy)
8. Blog-to-social repurposing chain and promotion/distribution
9. 90-day execution roadmap
10. KPI scorecard + evidence gaps + next 3 actions

## Quick Plan

Business snapshot; 3 customer problems; 3 query/page hypotheses; 5 distinct social concepts; first 7 days; 3 relevant KPIs.

## SEO Focus

Verifiable issue table (Observed / Needs checking), intent/keyword hypotheses, architecture/prioritized URL briefs, technical/GBP checks as appropriate, one ready-to-publish page outline, measurement. Do not fabricate ranking positions or site audits.

## Social Focus

Chosen channels, audience and message, 4–6 pillars, format reasoning, 4-week sustainable calendar, 3 complete scripts or carousel briefs when requested, engagement/community process, platform-specific KPIs.

## Single Asset

Deliver requested blog/reel/GBP post/caption/carousel/SEO metadata directly. Include minimal factual/proof notes; no unrelated giant strategy. Ask only indispensable missing information.

## Audit / Refresh

Evidence log (source + date), finding/uncertainty, impact, effort, recommendation, validation step, measurement baseline. If page/analytics not accessible, say **Not audited** and make a check-list.

## Copy QA

- Hooks clarify a specific relevant problem/question; not clickbait.
- Article content meets search intent, adds distinct evidence, does not claim a fixed “optimal word count”.
- Malayalam grammar and terminology are natural and accurate.
- Evidence + offer are verified; no unsupported rankings or results.
- If request includes a duration, budget/production resources or channel, respect it.
