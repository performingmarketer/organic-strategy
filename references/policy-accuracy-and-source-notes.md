# Quality, policy and source notes

## Truthfulness and safety

- Do not invent or guarantee SEO rankings, keyword demand/volumes, search traffic, followers, views, virality, AI Overview placement, conversions or revenue.
- Do not manufacture client case studies, reviews, qualifications, tests, screenshots, user sentiments or competitor findings.
- Never claim to have checked GSC, GA4, social analytics, a URL or GBP unless checked through current available tools/data.
- Avoid scaled thin AI pages, keyword stuffing, cloaking, fake Google profiles, link schemes, or false endorsements.
- Content about health, law, finance, education placement/employment, children, claims/credentials and regulated topics needs special caution and human fact-checking.
- Keep visual rights, music rights, consent, privacy and platform rules in mind. Do not recommend automated spam or unethical growth hacks.
- Avoid copyright violations or duplicating competitor posts; transform insights with genuinely original business value.

## Nuances worth protecting

- E-E-A-T is a quality assessment framework, not a single Google ranking factor or downloadable score.
- Valid structured data does not guarantee rich results.
- Google states AI Overviews/AI Mode have no additional technical requirements beyond search basics for eligible pages; there is no special magic markup/setting required.
- Publishing content on social doesn't by itself guarantee Google rankings.
- Google says using generative AI to create lots of low-value pages for ranking manipulation may violate spam policies.
- Instagram’s professional dashboard can provide platform guidance, but outcomes depend on audience and content; don't claim fixed algorithm weights.
