# SEO research, technical triage, local search and AI search

## Workflow

1. Inventory important pages from actual URL list/site crawl, Search Console export or user notes; if absent, mark URLs **proposed**.
2. Group customer questions by intent and journey. Do not force every query into a blog. Prioritize existing pages where possible.
3. Keyword hypotheses: query, intent, locale/language, business value, proposed destination, research needed, priority reasoning. Search volume/difficulty/actual rank must be `Unknown` until obtained from a credible tool; don't claim live search data without it.
4. Detect possible topic overlap; recommend one canonical primary page per core intent with natural internal links to supporting material.
5. Technical triage: discovery/indexing (robots.txt, noindex, sitemap, canonicals, HTTP, redirects); content discoverability (links/nav); mobile accessibility; usability/performance; structured data and rendering where relevant. Diagnose ONLY from observed access, tools or supplied crawl reports. If you cannot inspect, show a **verification checklist**, never 'site is slow' or 'sitemap missing'.
6. Content: accurate title/H1, plain-language answer, originality, firsthand proof, helpful imagery/alt, citations where needed, relevant links, clear action, update process. No magical word count, keyword density or title-character requirement.
7. Prioritize actions by **expected impact × confidence ÷ effort** conceptually (low/medium/high), not fake numeric scores or guaranteed growth.

## Local SEO module (only if relevant)

For a Kerala/local request use `local-seo-kerala-playbook.md` before making location or Maps recommendations: distinguish real staffed storefronts from service-area businesses and online-only operations; distinguish Maps ranking factors from organic search, and keep Malayalam/English query forms as **unverified hypotheses**.

- Verify real business eligibility and permitted Google Business Profile details; don't invent locations or service areas.
- Maintain truthful business name, category, hours, contact info, services, appointment path and photos; prompt for consented genuine reviews, never fake or incentivized reviews.
- Location and service pages must have genuinely location-specific substance, not cloned keyword doorway pages.
- Relevant third-party citations and community mentions should be earned, not purchased as fake links.
- Google Business Profile updates can inform customers, but never promise a direct rankings boost from post frequency.

## AI Overviews/AI Mode (Google-specific)

According to Google Search Central guidance, normal SEO fundamentals apply and there are **no additional technical requirements** or special markup to appear as supporting links in AI Overviews or AI Mode. Pages need to be indexable and eligible to appear with snippets; appearance is not guaranteed. Do not invent an “enable AI overview” toggle, hidden rank factor, special AEO schema, magic `llms.txt` requirement, or AI-citation guarantee. Suggest clear, original, well-sourced, firsthand content, accessible text and consistent product/business facts. For other AI platforms, avoid universal promises.

## Schema and structured data

Only suggest relevant currently supported types where visible page facts justify them; never create fabricated rating/review/FAQ or unsupported eligibility claims. Prefer JSON-LD with validated properties; check current Google support and test when tools permit. Even valid markup does not guarantee rich results.

## Technical SEO priority examples

- High: accidental `noindex` on important service page (**only if verified**), broken canonical to irrelevant URL, inaccessible critical pages.
- Medium: unclear internal links to a valuable service, duplicate query purpose, thin page missing unique proof.
- Low: cosmetic metadata polish with no strong customer-value hypothesis.

## Content quality gate

Every new page should answer a real query, add information unavailable from a generic AI summary (evidence/photos/examples/specific guidance), and have a reason to exist. Avoid high-volume thin AI pages, copied local variations, exact-match keyword stuffing, paid-link schemes and fake expertise.
