# Organic social strategy, distinct creative angles and reuse

## Platform-by-purpose (not fixed ranking advice)

- **Instagram:** visually legible Reels, carousels, genuine stories; clear hooks, relevant audio where rights permit, and useful captions. Review current professional-dashboard Best Practices where available.
- **Facebook:** helpful local/community posts, FAQs, event details, appropriate groups (respect their rules), customer interaction.
- **YouTube/Shorts:** demonstrable education/how-to, clear titles and context, watchable sequences; long form when topic depth deserves it.
- **LinkedIn:** professional insight, process breakdown, original experiences/case studies with verified evidence; B2B community.
- Other platforms only if audience and resources justify them. Do not assert a platform algorithm's internal weights.

## 6 possible pillars; customize instead of copying

| Pillar | Audience need | Format examples | Distinct value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pain point / problem diagnosis | understand why a challenge exists | 30s myth-busting reel | clarifies the real problem |
| Practical how-to / demonstration | learn an action | screen tutorial, before/after where genuine | original proof of process |
| Comparison / decision aid | select an approach | carousel, FAQs | makes tradeoffs clear |
| Credibility / proof | feel confident | consented review, project walkthrough | substantiates claims |
| Process / behind the scenes | understand delivery | team/process reel | trust and transparency |
| Community / follow-up | ask questions and stay engaged | story polls, customer FAQs | two-way learning |

## Reel template

**Audience & stage:** ...  **Problem:** ... **Unique message:** ... **Required evidence/shot:** ...

**Hooks (3, not near-identical):** question / surprising verified observation / how-to promise.

**Voiceover:** Full natural script matching requested seconds. **Visual beats:** opening problem, real example, demonstration, practical takeaway, relevant action. **On-screen words:** 1 short idea per shot. **Caption:** short SEO-aware plain-language context; hashtags only if relevant, not a ranking guarantee. **CTA:** profile visit / save / share / enquiry / guide, whichever genuinely serves the content.

## Carousel template

1. Cover with one clear question/problem.
2. Explain why it happens.
3–5. Offer original steps/examples or comparisons.
6. Highlight decision checklist.
7. Next action or concise takeaway.

Not every carousel needs seven slides; choose the number required to explain the subject.

## 30-day calendar guidance

Suggested columns: week, publish date/day, customer question, buyer stage, pillar, platform, format, hook/title, website/blog tie-in, asset/proof needed, CTA, owner, KPI, status. Create actual dates only when start date and locale/timezone are clear. Otherwise use Week 1–4 / Day 1–30. A calendar should include active creation and refresh/engagement days, not 30 forced posts.

## Repurposing chain

For substantive source-to-channel output, first complete `search-to-social-enquiry-engine.md` and `malayalam-content-quality.md` as appropriate: record source evidence; make the Reel demonstrate one thing, carousel answer a different decision question, and local/community post invite useful participation. Do not change facts or copy the same headline across formats.

One original customer insight + demonstration → service FAQ or blog → (a) short reel with true example (b) carousel decision aid (c) authentic story Q&A (d) short Facebook/LinkedIn context post where relevant (e) GBP update if useful and permitted. Keep distinct hooks, correct message for the audience and clear links; do not manufacture new claims.

## Editorial psychology

Attention → question/problem → specific understanding → evidence/value → next step. Avoid unverified “viral” predictions, fake FOMO, empty bait, “guaranteed results”, unlicensed media, misleading before/after footage, and irrelevant hashtags.

## Social hygiene

Respond helpfully to relevant comments/DMs; protect private data, respect opt-in and consent, follow community rules. Do not recommend fake followers, automatic follow-unfollow, spam replies, mass unsolicited DMs or engagement pods.
